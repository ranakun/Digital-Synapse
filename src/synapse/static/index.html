    About this map

    Every dot represents real material in your saved knowledge. The overview shows a connected sample from each area, with shown and total counts. Areas have comparable space; their visual size does not represent information volume.

    Area names are heuristic. Sample connections guide point placement, while area placement balances readability and cross-area links. Point size is uniform. Distance is not a strength or confidence score. Dotted lines show computed similarity or shared material; click a line to inspect its explanation.

    Recorded connections keep their evidence and review state. “Accepted” means included in your knowledge; it does not mean verified or endorsed by you.
